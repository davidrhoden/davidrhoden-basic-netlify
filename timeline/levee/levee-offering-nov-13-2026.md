I noticed this offering on the levee, it wasn't hidden, it was right by the walking path. I guess it's notable that there was noney in it, a $5 bill, cash. It stuck around for a couple days, until the third day I walked by and it was gone.

I imagine nothing very bad happens to you if you take money from a display like this, maybe nothing happens at all. Maybe it's intended that if the right person comes along needing five dollars, they can take it. I could definitely use five dollars, who couldn't, but not enough to disturb a display like this. Lightning won't strike, but it disrupts the intended energy. There'll be other five dollar bills.
