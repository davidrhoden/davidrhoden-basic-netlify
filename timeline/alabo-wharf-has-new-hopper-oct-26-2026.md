I thought the grain terminal project had halted, but it looks like it hasn't. This grain hopper appeared the other day. (I don't know what day, this was the first time I was up there to notice it.)

I haven't joined in the protests against having a grain terminal on the levee in our neighborhood, because I'm not against it. The wharft need to be in use. There's been a lot of complaint that it'll fill our neighborhood with grain dust and the train going through will vibrate everybody's houses down. Maybe they're right. My Destrehan friends talk about the part of their neighborhood where houses are cheap because they're close to the huge grain elevator Archer Daniels Midland has over there for offloading ships.
