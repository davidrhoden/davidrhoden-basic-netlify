![Printer's fist painting by David Rhoden](/static/img/paintings/printers-fist-oct-3-2026.jpeg)

First off, I need to emphasize this is based on someone else's drawing, I don't know whose. I found it on this fascinating website, [peculiarmanicule.com](https://peculiarmanicule.com) that has a wealth of peculiar manicules. (In typsetting, this type of pointing hand is called a manicule, or a printer's fist.)

So, not my drawing, but I've sometimes said, if you love a piece of artwork, try and make a copy for yourself. so I guess that's what I did here. I can't wait to hang it up. Not sure which direction it'll point (up, down, to the right?). Not sure what the title will be either. Or how much to sell it for.
