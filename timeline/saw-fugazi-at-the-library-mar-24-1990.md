This gig was at "Backstage at The Library", i.e., they didn't want these guys on the main stage. Whitey (very good local band) opened.
Problenm was, lotsa hillbilly white supremacists showed up. Not from Knoxville:; I mean, literally from the hiils. I'm not sure they fiinished the show. I feel like there was some disagreement between band and (some of the) audience about the coolness and appropriateness of "Roman salutes". It's pretty depressing to think about. It should have been awesome.
