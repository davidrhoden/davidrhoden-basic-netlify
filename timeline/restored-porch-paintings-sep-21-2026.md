These two paintings were really showing a lot of damage from living outside on my front porch, so, I scraped them down and repainted them. I used cheaper materials this time, just some paint I had around from home improvement projects. It's fun to see them looking good again though.

![scraped down KISS Guy, ready for repainting.](/static/img/paintings/kiss-guy-before-restoration-sep-18-2026.jpeg)

It wasn't as damaged as it might look here; this is after I scraped all the loose paint off.
