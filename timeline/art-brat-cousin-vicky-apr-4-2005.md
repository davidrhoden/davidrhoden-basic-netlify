There was so much going on at this party that I forgot the Stacks played. (Maybe we didn't?) The point of it was for people to swap art. I took something, probably a Startlebot. I came away with this cool stencil of chimps drinking wine by Chad Mo.

![stencil of chimps drinking wine by Chad Mo.](/static/img/timeline/chimp-stencil-by-chad-mo-apr-3-2004.jpeg)

But the think everyboy would remember was the debut (?) appearance of "Cousin Vicky". She was Tom Beeman's cousin from out of town. It was strange though, I never saw the two of them in the same room together. I hope they were getting along. Anyway, Cousin Vicky played her keyboards and sang her songs in a growly, masculine, hard-to-understand voice that was really novel and fresh.
