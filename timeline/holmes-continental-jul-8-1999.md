![mailer for Holmes show at Continental, July 8, 1999.](/static/img/timeline/holmes-continental-july-8-1999.png)

The Hi-Balls drummer, Shannon, was friends with these guys. The way she described them, I didn't think I would like them at all, but I saw them and they were great. I ended up becoming friends with them too. Their singer, Peter Gallagher, was and is the artist behind Heathcliff.
