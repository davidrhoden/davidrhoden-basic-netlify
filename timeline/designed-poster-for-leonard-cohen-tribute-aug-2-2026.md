I got asked to make a poster for this multi-act Leonard Cohen tribute coming up in September. I don't really know that much about Leonard Cohen (except I really don't like the ~~song~~ dirge "So Long, Maryanne"; sorry). So I looked up images, found a fine one; swiped a font choice from his oddly modern-looking _I'm Your Man_ album (I think I picked Microgramma over Eurostyle, was I correct?), tilted all the text, tilted Leonard so his eyeline would match the tilt of the text, and that's a wrap.

It was fun to do, but I don't know if Kinko's will even let someone print these, there's so much black.
