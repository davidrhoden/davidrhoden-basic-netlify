This is just a drawing, done during the Saints-Lions game of September 13, 2026, in which I was trying to understand the talent of a UK illustrator named [Charlotte Ager](https://charlotteager.co.uk/). I'm blown away by her work. I guess it looks pretty loose at first, but she is so good at capturing emotion and gesture. Her work is just wonderful to me. It's reminiscent of the work of [Quentin Blake](https://quentinblake.com) but doesn't feel like it's cribbed from him; she's got a whole personality of her own. I just like to see it when an artist draws in their most natural style and it's obviously _good_.

Take a look around her site, and note how correctly she gets proportions. [link to Charlotte Ager drawing](https://charlotteager.co.uk/?itemId=hd11ckju306fu5l8errggnky0wtnn5-6p7s7)
