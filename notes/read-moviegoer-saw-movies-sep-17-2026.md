I just re-read _The Moviegoer_ by Walker Percy. He mentions a dozen movies in the book, of which I’d seen eight. [(A Letterboxd list by "Justin" of the twelve movies mentioned.)](https://letterboxd.com/jmanthony/list/movies-mentioned-in-the-moviegoer-by-walker/) I've yet to see _Holiday_, _Fort Dobbs_, and _All Quiet On The Western Front_.

_Dark Waters_ (USA 1944, dir. Lazlo de Toth (the one-eyed director of _House of Wax (in 3D)_) was one I hadn’t seen. Got it from Internet Archive. 480p but good quality.
Stars Merle Oberon (who I’d never seen in a movie). Her story is strange—— she was part Chinese, I think? But she hid it. Sometimes she looks Chinese, and other times not at all. I can’t explain it better than that.

![Dark Waters poster with Merle Oberon, tagline "Have you ever been really afraid?"](/static/img/notes/dark-waters-really-afraid-sep-17-2026.jpg)

Anyway, her (the character, Leslie Calvin's) parents died in a shipwreck which she herself survived, but she has to go to a sanitarium in New Orleans. While there, she discovers her aunt and uncle, heretofore strangers, are living in a big plantation nearby. They invite her to come and stay...

![Dark Waters poster with Merle Oberon, Thomas Mitchell, and Elisha Cook Jr.](/static/img/notes/dark-waters-poster-w-villains-sep-17-2026.jpg)

The plantation is run by Mr. Sidney (Thomas Mitchell—Uncle Billy from _It’s a Wonderful Life_) and Mr. Cleve (Elisha Cook Jr.! From _The Maltese Falcon_ and _Blacula_!) Mr. Sidney has lots of rules. Mr. Cleve immediately starts sexually harassing Merle Oberon in the crudest, most obvious way, and never stops. Franchot Tone also stars as a kindly country doctor...but is he exactly what he seems?

Anyway, it’s a “B-picture”, but quite good. Exactly 90 minutes.

If you'd like to read a little more about the film, [here's a blog post by "Laura"](https://laurasmiscmusings.blogspot.com/2025/08/tonights-movie-dark-waters-1944-at-ucla.html) that describes it well.

![Dark Waters poster with Merle Oberon and Franchot Tone](/static/img/notes/dark-waters-poster-oberon-tone-sep-17-2026.jpg)

I started _The Young Philadelphians_ with Paul Newman, another movie mentioned in _The Moviegoer_. It is INSANE, the soapiest, most over-dramatic movie I think I’ve ever seen except maybe _The Group_. A little spoiler to illustrate this: The movie begins with the wedding of Kate Judson (Diane Brewster) and William Lawrence III (Adam West), the scion of a hoity-toity Main Line family. But at the honeymoon Adam West confesses he “CAN NEVER LOVE ANYONE!” (cause he’s probably a homosexual, it is implied) and a minute later in movie time, he is dead. He has five lines maybe but what a performance. He sounds exactly like Batman.

![poster for The Young Philadelphians featuring Diane Brewster and Adam West](/static/img/notes/the-young-philadelphians-poster-sep-17-2026.jpg)

I guess I should talk about _The Moviegoer_, but I'm not ready for that.
